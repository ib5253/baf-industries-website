Quality Mindset

Quality begins before fabrication. It starts with scope clarity, drawing review, material understanding, communication discipline, and practical execution checks.

Recognized Practices

Project teams can evaluate BAF against practical quality expectations and recognized HVAC construction guidance.

  • SMACNA awareness where applicable.
  • DW/144 awareness for duct construction, leakage, pressure classes, and installation expectations.
  • BS 476 Part 24 awareness for fire-rated ductwork requirements where project scope demands it.
  • MEP coordination aligned with contractor, consultant, procurement, and site workflows.
