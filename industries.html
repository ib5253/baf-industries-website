Industries Served

Controlled environments for today's modern building and facility HVAC needs.

BAF Industries provides HVAC, duct fabrication, installation, testing, and documentation support services for commercial, residential, hospitality, healthcare, pharma, industrial, kitchen exhaust, parking ventilation, and future thermal environments.

Commercial Residential Hospitality Healthcare & Pharma Industrial Kitchens Parking & Smoke Data Centers

Not every environment has the same demands from an HVAC perspective. Some need comfort, some require hygienic control, while others need smoke movement, heat removal, low-noise performance, or uptime. The goal is to understand the demands of the environment and then provide the HVAC ductwork, equipment connection, and documentation support to achieve those demands.

Office buildings, retail centers, mixed-use buildings, showrooms, and common commercial areas need HVAC systems that are coordinated, sustainable, and practical.

BAF supports commercial HVAC systems with PI and GI ducting, duct connections, fresh air and return air paths, ceiling ducts, shaft ducting, accessories, quantity takeoffs, and project documentation.

BAF supports kitchen exhaust systems with MS duct fabrication, grease exhaust ductwork, fire-rated duct coordination where necessary, access doors, exhaust fan ducting, make-up air ducting, and fabrication.
